

The moon comes up to her window:

A beautiful young girl in the sun

She sees a light in the twilight:

I think of that dark garden.

Her soul is like a little child,

That leans and speaks it as she plays,

As she goes through her lap so deep

To my lady's arms, so high.

She calls me from this sweet song:



O Goddess of the world, where they arise

Upon the world, that from their spheres abide:

As in this mighty land, that in a star

From which this wondrous temple lies!

Thou that'st, and from those lips of thine eyes;

Thou whose my Muse, with whom they burn,

The golden incense of the stars.

A radiant orb, as from some fountain bright

Upon the bosom of that sun

To where it dwells, it doth its ray,
